## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an AI-powered legal-fintech platform that safeguards Indian small business owners facing complex GST scrutiny notices (such as Form ASMT-10) and structures them into actionable, Chartered Accountant-ready briefs. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Calm Under Pressure:** Receivers of tax department notices experience sharp anxiety. The UI acts as a tranquilizer—replacing fear, bureaucratic chaos, and urgency-driven errors with clarity, order, and methodical composure.
- **Formidable Trust & Inviolable Defense:** Like its namesake (ढाल / Shield), the interface radiates structural integrity, defensive solidity, and institutional authority without feeling archaic or cold.
- **Indian-Modern Precision:** A refined bridge between traditional compliance rigor and sleek, high-efficiency modern software. It speaks natively to bilingual Indian business realities—clean English layout paradigms interleaved seamlessly with balanced Indic script rendering (Devanagari).

### Design Style Movement
The visual framework draws upon **Modern Institutional Precision** combined with **Tonal Functionalism**:
- Razor-sharp 1px structural framing, disciplined surface layers, deliberate whitespace, and mathematical proportion.
- High-signal functional color orchestration: deep navy foundations to anchor executive credibility, authoritative indigo anchors, and precise saffron highlights reserved strictly for statutory deadlines, mission-critical resolutions, and high-impact calls to action.
- Absolute rejection of frivolous decoration, ungrounded gradients, or arbitrary animations. Every line, badge, and monospace block signifies exactitude, evidentiary readiness, and audit capability.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative weight with accessible high-contrast scanning. The interface defaults to an expansive, high-legibility light theme engineered for daylight office work and rigorous document reviews.

### Primary, Secondary, & Highlight Archetypes
- **Deep Navy (`#0B1F3A`):** The primary bedrock. Embodies institutional gravity, sovereign compliance, and foundational containment. Used for key navigational pillars, top-tier headings, and structural anchors.
- **Accent Indigo (`#2B4C9B`):** The secondary functional voice. Drives structural actions, selected tabs, interactive controls, and focal links.
- **Highlight Saffron (`#F59E0B`):** The high-urgency beacon. Reserved for statutory deadlines, critical discrepancies between GSTR-1 vs GSTR-3B, impending hearing cut-offs, and paramount action buttons.
- **Verified Teal (`#0E9FA8`):** Serves as an evidentiary accent for validated cross-checks, authenticated GSTIN verification badges, and automated AI checksum match confirmations.

### Surface, Neutral, & Functional Canvas
- **Background (`#F6F8FB`):** Cool, glare-free tinted base providing contrast against white structural cards.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-purity white cards and panels that house scrutiny metrics, discrepancy tables, and CA brief drafts.
- **Border Neutral (`#E3E8F0`):** Precision 1px hairline boundary separating modules without visual noise.
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`):** Ultra-dark slate ensuring maximum contrast for dense compliance data.
- **Text Muted (`#5B6B82`):** Calm slate-neutral for field descriptions, historical dates, and sub-captions.

### Compliance & Status Signals
- **Success (`#12805C`):** Reconciled values, eligible ITC, verified submissions, and cleared demands.
- **Warning (`#B45309`):** Explanatory variances, missing supplier filings, and approaching threshold limits.
- **Danger (`#C0392B`):** Active tax demands under Section 73/74, unpaid interest, and immediate notice deadlines.

All foreground-to-background combinations meet or exceed WCAG AA 4.5:1 contrast metrics for standard text and 3:1 for graphical interface elements.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is engineered around a tripartite discipline: authoritative titles, high-density legible prose, and strict tabular-numerical fidelity.

### Font System Roles
- **Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern, clear geometric balance that projects confident guidance across viewports.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse engineered for complex UI interactions, legal explanations, and form layouts.
- **Codes & Data (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for technical compliance tokens: GSTIN identifiers (e.g., `27AAPFU0912K1ZV`), DIN (Document Identification Numbers), statutory sections (`Sec 16(2)(c)`), HSN/SAC codes, and monetary INR figures. Monospace formatting prevents visual misalignment in reconciliation grids.
- **Multilingual Support (Noto Sans Devanagari):** For dual-language translations (Hindi & Marathi notices/briefs), Noto Sans Devanagari is applied with an expanded line-height (`1.6` multiplier) to prevent matra clipping and maintain optical balance alongside Latin typography.

### Responsive Scaling
Display and large headline styles scale down on mobile viewports via designated tokens (`display-lg-mobile` and `headline-lg-mobile`), preventing awkward title wrapping in constrained screen contexts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a strict 8px spatial grid, emphasizing disciplined alignment and breathing room to counter high-density compliance stress.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid constrained to a maximum width of `1440px`. Column gutter is set to `1.5rem` (`24px`) with outer canvas margins of `2rem` (`32px`). Ideal for side-by-side verification: original scanned notice on the left pane, structured AI parsing and CA draft on the right pane.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.5rem` (`24px`) margins. Side-by-side panels convert into stacked or tabbed configurations.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1rem` (`16px`) margins. Sidebars reflow to a bottom sheet or off-canvas drawer. Primary discrepancy summaries and countdowns stay sticky above the fold.

### Component Spacing Rhythms
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-gaps between status badges, inline icons, and data labels.
- `space-sm` (8px): Standard compact gap between related form fields, table cell vertical padding, and breadcrumbs.
- `space-md` (16px): Core container padding inside cards, default layout element separation.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card header padding, separation between major form sections.
- `space-xl` (32px): Vertical distance between distinct notice analysis modules and briefing steps.
- `space-2xl` (48px): Major section dividers on dashboard overview canvases.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies primarily on layered structural surfaces, crisp 1px borders, and ultra-subtle tinted ambient shadows. Heavy skeuomorphism and blurry neomorphism are deliberately avoided to maintain official, audit-grade credibility.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** `#F6F8FB` — Tinted, anti-glare foundation.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Panels):** `#FFFFFF` — Encased with a 1px border of `#E3E8F0` and an ambient diffuse shadow (`0 1px 3px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.03)`).
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Surfaces & Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` — Framed by a 1px border of `#E3E8F0` and a crisp, mid-range elevation shadow (`0 4px 6px -1px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.06), 0 10px 24px -3px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.08)`).
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` — Backed by a backdrop scrim of `#0B1F3A` at `40% opacity` with an elevation shadow (`0 20px 35px -5px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.16), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.04)`).

### Elevation Rules
- Interactive elements elevate by one shadow tier upon hover, coupled with a border transition to `#2B4C9B`.
- Discrepancy callouts and high-priority deadline notices employ a 3px solid left accent border (e.g., `#F59E0B` for deadlines or `#C0392B` for tax demand discrepancies) rather than heavy elevation jumps.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, balanced geometry that avoids extreme pill shapes or cold, aggressive sharp corners.

### Shape Geometry Standards
- **Standard Controls & Elements (`roundedness: 2` = 8px):** Applied to form text inputs, standard buttons, tabs, table wrappers, and inline code blocks.
- **Cards & Data Modules (`rounded-lg` = 12px / 0.75rem):** Applied to ASMT-10 parsing cards, ITC discrepancy comparison panels, and brief preview containers.
- **Modals, Dialogs, & Drawers (`rounded-xl` = 16px / 1rem):** Applied to full-screen overlay dialogs, document upload zones, and notice verification modals.
- **Badges, Status Chips, & Monospace Tags:** Utilize a constrained `4px` or `6px` radius to reflect systematic, ticket-like precision. Fully circular shapes are restricted strictly to user avatars and step progression numbers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Deadline / High-Impact:** Solid Saffron (`#F59E0B`) with dark text (`#0F172A`), 8px radius, medium font weight. Used for "Generate CA Brief", "Download Form ASMT-11 Draft", and statutory response deadlines.
- **Primary Brand:** Deep Navy (`#0B1F3A`) background with white text (`#FFFFFF`), 8px radius. Used for "Upload ASMT-10 Notice", "Reconcile Data", and modal confirmation actions.
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined 1px `#E3E8F0` on white surface, `#0F172A` text; on hover, shifts to `#2B4C9B` border and subtle `#F6F8FB` fill.
- **Destructive Action:** Border or fill in `#C0392B` with high-contrast text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicators:** 4px radius, `JetBrains Mono` font at 11px uppercase with 0.04em tracking.
  - *Under Scrutiny:* Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border 1px `#FDE68A`.
  - *ITC Mismatch Found:* Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#C0392B`, border 1px `#FECACA`.
  - *Verified / Reconciled:* Background `#E6F4EA`, text `#12805C`, border 1px `#A8DAB5`.
  - *GSTIN / Section Tag:* Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#0B1F3A`, border 1px `#CBD5E1`.

### Data Comparison Lists & Tables
- **Reconciliation Grids:** Two-tier column comparison (e.g., "Values as per GSTR-3B" vs "Values as per GSTR-2A/2B"). Alternating subtle fills on hover, 1px `#E3E8F0` horizontal divider rules.
- **Numerical Cells:** Right-aligned, formatted with `JetBrains Mono` with uniform tabular lining figures to ensure decimal points align across lakhs and crores.

### Form Inputs & Upload Zones
- **Text Inputs:** White background, 1px border `#E3E8F0`, 8px radius, 14px `Inter`. Focus state introduces a crisp 2px ring in `#2B4C9B` without heavy glow.
- **Dropzone (Notice OCR Ingestion):** 2px dashed border in `#2B4C9B`, subtle background tint `#F6F8FB`, rounded-lg (12px). Features an instant parsing progress indicator showing extracted GSTIN, Tax Period, Notice Reference, and Demand Amount.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 8px/circular controls with 1.5px border `#5B6B82`. Active state fills with Deep Navy (`#0B1F3A`) and white checkmark icon.

### Cards & CA Brief Containers
- White `#FFFFFF` surface, 12px radius, 1px border `#E3E8F0`. 
- Header includes a dedicated title, DIN/Notice ID badge in `JetBrains Mono`, and days-remaining deadline pill (`#F59E0B`).
- Structured in two parts: *Discrepancy Breakdown* on top, followed by an expandable *Recommended Legal Reply & Statutory Proviso* below.
