## 🎬 Minute-by-Minute Script

### 🕒 00:00 – 00:30 | Step 1: Landing & Upload
- **What to say:**
  > *"Judges, when a small business owner in India receives a GST scrutiny notice (Form ASMT-10), their immediate reaction is panic. They see huge numbers, complicated sections like Rule 99, and think their bank accounts are about to be frozen. Meet **DHAAL (ढाल)**: their statutory notice shield. Within 5 seconds, it replaces fear with institutional order."*
- **Click Action:**
  1. Point to the top bar: persistent disclaimer *"Statutory Drafting aid for professional review. Not legal advice. Nothing is filed."*
  2. Point to the Hindi tagline: **"नोटिस आया? ढाल है ना।"**
  3. Click **"Quick Test: Load Sample ASMT-10 Notice"** (or **"Try a sample notice"**).
  4. Show the animated deterministic parser running from 0% to 100% across the OCR, field detection, and pause checkpoint stages.

---

### 🕒 00:30 – 01:00 | Step 2: The Amber Pause Checkpoint
- **What to say:**
  > *"Here is DHAAL's defining architectural choice: **The Amber Pause State**. LLMs love to hallucinate numbers when document scans have ink smudges or printhead tears. Under tax law, declaring the wrong amount in a reply can count as an unintended tax admission. So DHAAL halts the pipeline."*
- **Click Action:**
  1. Point out the left side: the authentic vector-rendered Government of Maharashtra Form ASMT-10 notice.
  2. Point to Row 02: notice the smudge on digit `4` in ₹1,84,250.
  3. Point out the 4.8x magnified micro-scan crop: Primary Engine reads `₹1,84,250` vs Verification Pass `₹1,81,250` (a ₹3,000 delta).
  4. **The Live Test:** Type `181250` into the input and click **"Confirm and continue"**.
     - *Show the inline error:* *"This does not match the notice table (₹14,92,450 − ₹13,08,200 = ₹1,84,250). Please re-check the highlighted cell."*
  5. Click **"Match Read A (₹1,84,250)"** and click **"Refer GSTR-2B Math"** to show the mathematical proof.
  6. Click **"Confirm and continue"** → The banner turns green *"Confirmed by you"*, unlocking Step 3!

---

### 🕒 01:00 – 01:45 | Step 3: Explainer & Statutory Deadlines
- **What to say:**
  > *"Next, DHAAL answers the business owner's two most critical questions: 'How much time do I have?' and 'What is the officer actually accusing me of?' in their own mother tongue."*
- **Click Action:**
  1. Point out the **T-Minus 12 Days Left** countdown ring with the Saffron Caution Phase indicator.
  2. Drag the **"Preview urgency"** slider: scrub to 5 days to show the Critical Red phase, scrub to 25 days to show the Normal phase, then return to 12 days.
  3. Switch the language tabs:
     - Click **"हिन्दी"** → Show the Devanagari explanation (*"जीएसटी अधिकारी ने पाया कि आपने पहली तिमाही (अप्रैल-जून 2024)..."*) with zero clipped matras.
     - Click **"मराठी"** → Show the Marathi explanation (*"जीएसटी अधिकाऱ्यांच्या निदर्शनास आले आहे की..."*).
  4. Click the **Audio Speaker icon** (`volume_up`) to play native voice audio synthesized via Web Speech API.
  5. Hover over the explanation sentence to highlight the verbatim legal quote below (*Para 2(b) • Row 02*).
  6. Click **"View Raw Scan"** to pop open the modal with zoom controls, demonstrating full traceability back to the original document.
  7. Point to the **Clarity Checklist**: *"What this notice IS (an invitation to explain) vs What this notice IS NOT (NOT a demand order, accounts are NOT frozen)"*.
  8. Click **"Proceed to Evidence Checklist"**.

---

### 🕒 01:45 – 02:15 | Step 4: Evidence Checklist & Audit Trail
- **What to say:**
  > *"A Chartered Accountant cannot defend a client without an evidentiary binder fulfilling all four clauses of Section 16(2). DHAAL itemizes exactly what exhibits are needed."*
- **Click Action:**
  1. Show the 4 summary cards: Amount questioned (₹1,84,250), Bank Audit (100% cleared, Sample Bank), Window (12 days), and live Sufficiency Progress (50%).
  2. Click **"Download Sheet (CSV)"** on Item 3:
     - *Open or observe the downloaded CSV:* 24 invoice-level rows from supplier M/s Apex Textiles whose tax difference sums up to **exactly ₹1,84,250.00**.
  3. Click **"Upload Supplier Certificate"** on Item 4: show how the status immediately updates to *Uploaded (User Verified)* and the progress bar climbs live.
  4. Click **"Send 1-Click WhatsApp Request"** to show the pre-formatted supplier communication citing CBIC Circular 183.
  5. Click **"Continue to Draft & Verifier"**.

---

### 🕒 02:15 – 02:45 | Step 5: Draft & Dual-Layer Verifier
- **What to say:**
  > *"Now we synthesize the formal statutory Form ASMT-11 reply. But before any CA sees it, DHAAL runs a dual-layer verifier. If even a single number or date diverges from confirmed records, export is hard-blocked."*
- **Click Action:**
  1. Review the left column: Formal Form GST ASMT-11 draft with origin source chips (*Notice Field Verified*, *Step 2 Value*, *Sec 16(2)*) and amber *"Owner to confirm"* chips.
  2. Hover over the source chips: see the respective draft paragraphs illuminate.
  3. Point to the Right column: **Layer 1 Code Checks** (Amount, Sections, Dates, GSTIN, Cross-reconciliation) running through pure code regex in `verifier.ts`.
  4. **The Dramatic Test:** Click the mode toggle: **"Mismatch Detected (Blocked)"**.
     - Notice the full-width red alert banner: **"EXPORT HARD-BLOCKED: Statutory Discrepancy Integrity Violated"**.
     - Point out the exact mismatch: *"Draft asserts ₹1,81,250; confirmed value is ₹1,84,250"*.
     - Show that the *"Proceed to CA Brief"* button is disabled with a lock icon.
  5. Click **"Re-align Figures (Auto-fix)"**!
     - Watch the numbers restore to ₹1,84,250, the checks animate back to green, the banner clear, and the export button unlock!
  6. Click **"Proceed to CA Brief"**.

---

  3. Click **"Print"** to showcase the print stylesheet showing only the clean A4 brief.
  4. Click **"Send CA Pack"** to show the WhatsApp handoff modal with pre-filled case reference and access passcode (`DH-8912-PASS`).
- **Concluding Line:**
  > *"DHAAL is fast, honest, 100% offline-ready, and brings enterprise-grade compliance defense to every small business in India. Thank you!"*
