What the Officer Is Saying (English)

"The GST officer noticed that you claimed ₹1,84,250 more in Input Tax Credit (ITC) on GSTR-3B than what your suppliers reported in GSTR-2B for Q1 (Apr–Jun 2025)."

Under GST provisions, every rupee of tax rebate you take must match the exact invoices uploaded by your vendors. The department is questioning this ₹1,84,250 gap before considering it an unlawful tax evasion.

"जीएसटी अधिकारी ने पाया कि आपने पहली तिमाही (अप्रैल-जून 2025) के लिए GSTR-3B में आपके सप्लायर्स द्वारा GSTR-2B में दिखाए गए इनपुट टैक्स क्रेडिट (ITC) से ₹1,84,250 अधिक क्लेम किया है।"
